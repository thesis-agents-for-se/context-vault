## configuration-judge - configuration - run ff3b0c46 (unapproved proposal)

### Service Cutter Configuration Analysis

This [[systems/soccer-mybatis/architecture/runs/ff3b0c46|run ff3b0c46]] documents the evaluation and selection of a configuration proposal for Service Cutter, focusing on the decomposition of the system into microservices. The analysis compared three proposals: **business**, **code**, and **risk**, with the latter being selected as the most robust and evidence-backed.

### Key Observations
1. **Evidence-Based Decision Making**
   - The **risk** proposal was chosen for its explicit treatment of uncertainty, alignment with business invariants, and focus on security risks. It leverages metrics such as:
     - [[metric:consistency_criticality]] (E388-E396) to identify non-confluent pairs in critical operations.
     - [[metric:structural_volatility]] (E384-E387) and [[metric:content_volatility]] (E398-E403) to estimate stability.
     - [[metric:security_classification]] (E381) to classify sensitive data.

2. **Handling of Critical Operations**
   - The proposal prioritizes operations like:
     - [[systems/soccer-mybatis/business/use-cases/delete-team|delete_team]] (E330, E331), which requires cascading deletion of players to maintain business invariants.
     - [[systems/soccer-mybatis/business/use-cases/scoregoal|scoreGoal]] (E376, E377), which links `matches` and `goals` to ensure data consistency.

3. **Security and Data Sensitivity**
   - Entities like [[systems/soccer-mybatis/code/entities/teams|teams]] are classified as **Internal** due to containing PII (e.g., city, country), aligning with PCI DSS and NIST SP 800-122 (E381). This justifies the high weight assigned to **Security Contextuality** (value 8) and **Security Criticality** (value 5).

4. **Disagreements Among Proposals**
   - The **code** proposal underestimated **Consistency Criticality**, assigning it a value of 5 despite evidence of non-confluent pairs in critical operations.
   - The **business** proposal overvalued **Security Constraint** (value 8) without sufficient evidence of compliance requirements or audits.

### Limitations
- **Absence of Telemetry and SLOs**: The lack of historical data on latency, availability, or volatility limits the confidence in criteria like **Latency** and **Availability Criticality**.
- **Undetermined Invariants**: Metrics like [[metric:consistency_criticality]] (E397) identified signs of uniqueness in `competition_teams`, but these cannot be statically validated.
- **Security Classification**: The reliance on naming rules for security (e.g., PCI DSS, NIST SP 800-122) may not account for contextual nuances in the data.
