Soccer league - How the system is
used
Written for testing. Based on what the code of the soccer sample does.
Who uses it
League staﬀ: they register teams and players, create matches
and competitions, and record what happens in a match.
Administrators: they do what the staﬀ do and, in addition, delete
teams and reset the goals of a team.
Other systems: they read teams, players and matches.
Before the season
The staﬀ create each team with its city, country and coach.
They add the players of each team, one by one.
They create the competitions of the season and register each
team in the ones it will play.
If a player leaves, the staﬀ remove the player from the team by
the shirt number.
On match day
The staﬀ create the match with the two teams and the stadium.
During the match, each goal is recorded as it happens: the team,
the shirt number of the scorer and the minute.
Anyone can ask for the match at any time to see the scoreboard.
When the referee ends the match, the staﬀ ﬁnish it. After that the
result does not change.
After the match
The team page shows how many players the team has, their
average age and the top scorer.
The list of players can be ﬁltered by position.
What must be fast (assumptions)

This part is not in the code. It was written for the tests of this
repository and should be conﬁrmed by a person who knows the
league.
Recording a goal should feel immediate: it happens live and the
person recording it cannot wait.
Showing the scoreboard of a match should be fast: many readers
ask for it during a match.
Registering teams, players and competitions is done before the
season, by a few people. It does not need to be fast.
What belongs together (assumptions)
This part is not in the code either.
The squad of a team is managed by one group of people, before
the season.
Matches and their goals are managed by another group, on
match day.
Competitions change once a season.
